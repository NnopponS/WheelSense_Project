WheelSense / EaseAI — Facility room changes for Barthel ADL
============================================================
Purpose of this file: lock the room map BEFORE regenerating the
simulated timeline. Do not score Barthel yet. The old CSV
(sim_patient_timelines.csv) used rooms as a random-walk graph.
That cannot represent a care-home day or the Collin & Wade
Barthel Index (0-20, three tiers).


1. Why the rooms must change
----------------------------
Room nodes only answer WHERE a resident is. Barthel answers WHAT
they did and HOW MUCH HELP they needed.

The current map fails because:
  - Room 401-406 are treated as interchangeable wander space.
    Residents do not live in other people's bedrooms.
  - A single "Bathroom" mixes toilet use, bowel/bladder, and
    bathing. Those are different Barthel items.
  - Dining, PT, lounge, and nurses' station are used as 30-90 s
    hops, not as real destinations.
  - There is no 24-hour occupancy in a home bedroom (sleep).

Rule: each Barthel item that location can support must have a
place it can appear as occupancy. Items that happen inside one
room (transfers, dressing, tray meals) use the HOME BEDROOM.

5. How rooms map to Barthel items (0-20, Collin & Wade)
-------------------------------------------------------
  Item              pts    Visible in which room
  Bowels            0-2    Toilet (+ night in home bedroom)
  Bladder           0-2    Toilet (+ night in home bedroom)
  Grooming          0-1    Home bedroom morning block (inferred)
  Toilet use        0-2    Toilet (self-initiated trip + dwell)
  Feeding           0-2    Dining Room; tray meal = stay in home bedroom
  Transfers         0-3    Home bedroom / Toilet — NOT a room change
  Mobility          0-3    Main Hall + destinations; wheelchair independent = 1
  Dressing          0-2    Home bedroom after wake, before breakfast
  Stairs            0-2    Not in this facility; score 0 for chair users
  Bathing           0-1    Shower (long morning block, few times / week)

Location can SUPPORT toilet, bathing, feeding opportunity, and
mobility. It cannot score transfer quality, true continence,
chewing/being fed, or dressing skill. Those need staff/IMU later.

7. How the three tiers USE these rooms (timeline grammar)
---------------------------------------------------------
Tiers are Collin & Wade totals (not computed in this step):
  Tier 1  12-19  Independent / mild dependence
  Tier 2   5-11  Moderate dependence
  Tier 3   0-4   Severe / total dependence

A wheelchair user who is otherwise independent often lands ~14-17
(mobility = 1, stairs = 0), still Tier 1.

Tier 1 — home is a base, not a cage
  Home -> Toilet -> Home (morning care) -> Hall -> Dining (all 3 meals)
  -> Toilet after meals -> PT and/or Lounge -> Shower a few times/week
  -> sleep in HOME room. Night: 0-1 Toilet. No other bedrooms.
  Room changes have empty caregiver_id (self-propelled).

Tier 2 — same rooms, escorted and shorter
  Some meals in Dining with caregiver_id; some tray in Home.
  Toilet clustered after meals (prompted), longer dwells.
  Shower with staff. Afternoon rest in Home. Little Lounge.
  Fewer metres through Hall.

Tier 3 — almost only home room
  Home bedroom most of 24 h. Meals in room (no Dining).
  No self Toilet, or 0-1 staff-taken trip. No self Shower
  (bed bath is invisible). PT/Lounge absent or rare.
  Absence of rooms is the signal.


8. Timeline rules that follow from this map
-------------------------------------------
  1. One resident, one room, all 24 hours (occupancy tape).
  2. Assigned home room for sleep and rest.
  3. Paths go Home <-> Hall <-> destination. No teleport.
  4. No duplicate echo enter/exit with empty data.
  5. Tag each interval with intended activity
     (sleep, toilet, shower, breakfast, transit, therapy, ...).
     That label is simulation ground truth for later Barthel.
  6. Do not compute the 0-20 score until this map and a clean
     day tape exist.
